Before you start

These prompts are creative starters — not finished products. AI is fast at generating ideas, slow at knowing your students. Use what comes back as inspiration, then refine.

Test and adjust. If the first output isn't quite right, edit the prompt and run it again. Specificity in → specificity out. Small wording changes change the output a lot.

Upload context for stronger results. Most prompts work better when Copilot has something to look at — your current syllabus, an assignment prompt, a draft, even a screenshot of student work. Drag the file into the chat before submitting the prompt, and Copilot will apply the prompt to your actual material rather than placeholder text.

Stay in the driver's seat. Some prompts ask Copilot to ask you clarifying questions first. Answer them honestly — that's where your pedagogical voice enters. Don't let Copilot skip ahead.
